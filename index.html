What we buy

Vacant land, anywhere in Madison County

We're looking for land the owner is ready to part with. It doesn't need to be cleared, surveyed or listed.

  • Tracts of about 5 to 150 acres
  • Inherited or family land, including land shared by several heirs
  • Land you've owned for years and rarely visit
  • Wooded, overgrown or never-developed land
  • Owners who live out of the area or out of state
  • Near Marshall, Mars Hill, Hot Springs or anywhere in between

How it works

Three steps, start to finish

1

Reach out

Email the parcel number or a rough location, and anything you'd like us to know. There's nothing to prepare.

2

Get a written cash offer

We research the land and send a clear cash offer in writing. If it doesn't work for you, no hard feelings.

3

Close with an attorney

A North Carolina attorney handles the contract and closing. We check the survey and title along the way, and you're paid at closing.
